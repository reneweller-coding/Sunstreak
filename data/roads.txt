# The customs of the roads, one per line: a keyword, then the countries (as data/countries.txt writes them).
#   mph | ...            speed limits in miles per hour (else km/h)
#   board | ...          speed limits on a white board (else in a red ring)
#   tolls | ...          toll plazas across the roads
#   cameras | ...        speed cameras: they flash, and cost time
#   plates STYLE | ...   number plates: EU (white with a blue band), UK (yellow behind), US (white, a coloured top),
#                        YELLOW, BLACK, JP (white with green letters); the rest white
# Cobbled old towns say "cobbles" in data/places.
#
# How the roads are built and kept:
#   quality N | ...      1..7 how good the roads are (the WEF's quality of road infrastructure, 2019; the countries
#                        it did not survey are estimated). Below 5 the markings fade, patches and potholes come;
#                        a country without a line has 4.5
#   width M | ...        metres per lane (else 3.5)
#   shoulders | ...      a paved shoulder beyond the edge line (else the verge right after it)
#   kerbs RRGGBB RRGGBB | ...   the painted kerbs of its towns (else plain grey stone)
#   studs | ...          reflecting road studs (cat's eyes) along the lines
#   concrete PCT [LANES] | ...  the share of its places paved with concrete slabs (only roads of LANES lanes or more)
#   gravel PCT | ...     the share of a country road that runs on gravel or dirt (a place's "gravel" step decides
#                        for itself)
# Places without a country keep the racing kerbs, and so do the places that paint their own.
