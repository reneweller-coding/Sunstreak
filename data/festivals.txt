# Festivals: they come on their dates (month-day, the first and the last; a span may run over the new year) to the
# places named after them: countries, COUNTRY/REGION, "north 60" (every place that far north) or * (everywhere).
#
#   festival NAME | A LINE ABOUT IT | MM-DD MM-DD | WHERE, WHERE, ...
#
# and bring, one per line, with their colours (RRGGBB):
#   bunting C C ...      strings of flags across the road
#   lanterns C C ...     strings of glowing lanterns across the road
#   lights C             strings of fairy lights across the road
#   lamps C              little lamps all along both edges of the road
#   stalls ROOF WALL     market stalls beside the road (data/landmarks MARKET_STALL)
#   tents ROOF STRIPE    beer tents beside the road (BEER_TENT)
#   trees                Christmas trees with their lights (CHRISTMAS_TREE)
#   confetti C C ...     colour in the air
#   fireworks C C ...    fireworks at night
#   aurora               the northern lights at night
# The first festival on a place's date wins.
