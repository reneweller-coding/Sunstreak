# Sunstreak release notes

## 1.0.0

The first public release: an arcade racer round the whole world, for Windows, Linux and the Steam Deck.

- **The world tour:** 781 places in all 202 countries and territories, the big countries state by state, each with its
  landscape, landmarks, houses, roadside stands, wildlife, real climate, sun and smog. At the borders the road forks;
  between landmasses a glass tunnel on the sea floor, a ferry or the car train. The **random tour** (Settings >
  SCENERY) leaps to another part of the world after every stage, each country once a round.
- **Real heights:** the road climbs and falls between the places to their real height, from the Dead Sea to the
  Himalaya; 28 legendary roads, the passes climbing in numbered hairpins to the height on their sign.
- **Every country drives its own way:** side, markings, signs and place names in their own scripts, number plates,
  speed cameras, tolls, police, ambulances, traffic, road quality, poles, lamps and guard rails.
- **Life on the road:** hitchhikers and courier jobs, local heroes, police chases, fast trains, landing airliners,
  festivals, monsoon, snow and wet roads.
- **Modes:** the endless run against the clock, travel without a clock, the daily challenge, rallies and journeys, a
  championship against eleven rivals, the Track Lab with shareable codes and ghosts.
- **Sound:** thirty-one radio stations composing as you listen, engines of four, eight and twelve cylinders, your own
  music as the station MY MUSIC.
- **69 awards**, the passport with its map, stamps, regions, postcards and album, a manual of 16 chapters.
