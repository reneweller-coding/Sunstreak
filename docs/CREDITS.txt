================================

from comes from these:

Fonts
-----
- The place names in their own scripts are traced from the Noto fonts, Copyright the Noto Project Authors, under
  the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL-Noto.txt beside this file); so are the traced strokes.

Data
----
- The roadside of the places (data/roadside.txt: the poles of the power lines, the street lamps, the guard rails,
  the gravel and concrete roads) is derived from OpenStreetMap data, (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, under the Open
  Database License (ODbL) 1.0, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright. The derived database data/roadside.txt is
  available under the ODbL as well.
- The heights of the places (data/altitudes.txt): ASTER GDEM, a product of METI and NASA, through the OpenTopoData
  API (https://www.opentopodata.org).
- The outlines of the passport's map: Natural Earth (https://www.naturalearthdata.com), public domain.
- The climate of the places (data/climate.txt): NASA POWER, thirty-year climatology. These data were obtained from
  the NASA Langley Research Center (LaRC) POWER Project funded through the NASA Earth Science/Applied Science
  Program (https://power.larc.nasa.gov).
- The smog through the year and the day (data/smog.txt): Open-Meteo Air Quality (https://open-meteo.com, CC BY 4.0),
  which contains modified Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service information 2024.
- The quality of the roads: World Economic Forum, Global Competitiveness Report 2019.
- The traffic and the pollution of the countries and cities: Numbeo (https://www.numbeo.com), traffic index and
  pollution index.

All drivers, local heroes and hitchhikers are fictional.
